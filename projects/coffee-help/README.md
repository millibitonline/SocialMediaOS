# Coffee.help

**Project** in SocialMediaOS · **Team:** Channels · **Status:** planning

Coffee.help is an educational media brand about coffee culture and brewing,
coffee science, polyphenols and botanical ingredients, flavor exploration, and
coffee rituals and experimentation.

Coffee.help is **affiliated with PolyCoffee**, an independent coffee
product-development business. PolyCoffee keeps authority over product strategy,
formulation, pricing, commercialization, and product claims. SocialMediaOS runs
social strategy, creative production, distribution, and analytics
([EXTERNAL_AUTHORITY.md](EXTERNAL_AUTHORITY.md)).

> No production, publishing, or integration authorization has been granted.
> Accounts are registered, not connected. No content has been created.
