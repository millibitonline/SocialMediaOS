## 1. Business

PolyCoffee develops **botanical coffee enhancements** intended to complement
people's existing coffee routines.

**Core proposition:** Make the coffee people already drink more interesting
without requiring them to change how they prepare it.

**Product development priorities**
- Diversity of plant-derived compounds, including polyphenols
- Subtle complementary flavors
- Compatibility with conventional drip brewing
- Convenience and simplicity
- Ingredient transparency
- Responsible scientific substantiation

> **Formulations and product efficacy remain unvalidated.**

## 2. Customer segments (PolyCoffee)

| Priority | Segment | Motivation |
|----------|---------|------------|
| Primary | Health-conscious coffee drinkers | Interested in nutrition, plant diversity, and wellness habits |
| Secondary | Coffee enthusiasts | Interested in flavor, brewing, and experimentation |
| Tertiary | Convenience-oriented consumers | Want additions that fit existing routines |

**Avoid targeting consumers seeking treatment for medical conditions.**

These are PolyCoffee's customer segments. Coffee.help's audience definition
belongs in [STRATEGY.md](STRATEGY.md) (strategist). It may overlap with these
segments, but it is set for educational value, not to fit product marketing.

## 4. Marketing objectives

| Phase | Goal | Metrics |
|-------|------|---------|
| **1. Audience development** *(current priority)* | Build a credible educational audience around coffee | Reach · Subscribers · Engagement · Returning viewers · Content retention |
| **2. Product education and learning** | Understand audience interest in botanical coffee enhancements | Educational content engagement · Surveys and audience feedback · Website visits · Waitlist participation |
| **3. Commercial conversion** | Introduce validated, approved products when commercially ready | Conversion rate · Customer acquisition cost · Revenue · Repeat purchases |

**Current priority: Phase 1.**

Mapping to the content classes in [CLAIMS_POLICY.md §2](CLAIMS_POLICY.md#2-content-classes-three-tier-review):
Phase 1 is Class A/B education. Phase 2 product-interest research that names
or implies PolyCoffee or its products is Class C. Phase 3 is Class C throughout.

## 7. Claims and scientific integrity

**No product-specific health or performance claims are currently approved.**
The approved-claims registry mirror
([claims/APPROVED_CLAIMS.yaml](claims/APPROVED_CLAIMS.yaml)) is empty.

**Permitted educational topics, with appropriate evidence**
- Coffee contains polyphenols.
- Cocoa contains flavanols.
- Scientific research into coffee and health.
- Brewing techniques and flavor chemistry.

These are general educational statements (Class A, or Class B when they touch
health). They are not product claims and must not be tied to a PolyCoffee product.

**Not approved**
- Claims that PolyCoffee improves immunity.
- Claims that PolyCoffee improves cognition.
- Claims that PolyCoffee prevents disease.
- Any unverified product composition or performance statement.

**Ingredient-level scientific findings must not be represented as demonstrated
benefits of a finished product.**

Follow [CLAIMS_POLICY.md](CLAIMS_POLICY.md) and the approved-claims registry.
